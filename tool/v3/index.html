This fund’s record

The fund

Bring your own file. Nothing is fetched and nothing is sent.

Which file, and where to get it
Which file
The daily NAV history of the scheme you own, from its first day to today. One scheme is enough; a file with every scheme of the fund house is fine.
Where
Your fund house’s website — search it for NAV history — or AMFI in 90-day pieces dropped here together.
Dates
Launch to today. If you are unsure, the earliest the site allows.
Format
Whatever it gives you. Screenshots and PDFs do not work.
Then
Tap Load, choose the files, and check the name and dates shown back to you.
An index to compare against
niftyindices.com → Reports → Historical Data → Total Returns Index, the index named on your factsheet, the widest dates allowed, in pieces if it refuses a long range. Same source, same steps, both times.
