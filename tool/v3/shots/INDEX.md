# The screenshot set

Generated by `node tools/v3/shoot.js`. Twenty shots at 390×844 and twice
that in pixels, which is a phone: six screens showing their readings, and
four more showing the door a ledger comes in through, each on both sheets.

**Shots 07–10 hold made-up transactions**, whether or not the NAV history
behind them is real, and are captioned as such.

**The series in these shots is generated, not a fund.** No figure in them
describes anything that happened. Re-run with `--fund <file>` against an
official NAV history and the same command produces the real-data set.
