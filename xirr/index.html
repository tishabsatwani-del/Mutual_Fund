The XIRR Sheet

Your own return, worked out properly, in about two minutes.

Your app already shows an XIRR for the funds you hold with it. This sheet is for everything it cannot see: money spread across two platforms, money you have already taken out, or a record you want to keep yourself.

Download the sheet

Excel file (.xlsx), 46 KB

On a phone you will need a free spreadsheet app, such as Google Sheets, to open it.

How to use it

  1. Download the file and open it in Excel. Save your own copy first, so you always have a clean one.
  2. On the My investments tab, enter one row for every payment you have made. Date, then choose Investment from the dropdown, then the amount. Always type the amount as a plain positive number.
  3. If you have taken money out, enter that as a row too, choosing Withdrawal.
  4. In the last row, enter today's date, choose Value today, and type what the holding is worth right now.

Your XIRR appears at the top of the sheet. That is the yearly rate your own money has earned.

If something looks wrong

  • No answer yet. Read the line under the result. It will tell you what is still missing, usually the Value today row.
  • The date looks odd. The sheet shows dates as 05-Aug-2026. If yours reads 08-May-2026, your computer is set to American dates. Retype the date the other way round.
  • A number will not go in. Type digits only, with no rupee sign and no commas.
  • No Excel? Upload the file to Google Sheets and it works there too, unchanged.

This is a free tool for readers, not investment advice. The figure it produces is before exit load and before tax, so what you finally keep will be a little less.
